FinGuru KYC Guidelines

Know Your Customer:
KYC processes are used to establish and verify customer identity.

Personal Information:
Sensitive personal information should only be collected and processed for an appropriate and permitted purpose.

Data Minimization:
Only information necessary for the intended financial service or analysis should be used.

Consent:
Where consent is required, customer data should not be used for a purpose outside the granted consent.

Privacy:
Sensitive information such as account numbers, phone numbers, email addresses, and identity information should be protected from unnecessary exposure.

Security:
Customer information should be protected through appropriate access controls, masking, and secure processing.
