FinGuru Financial Literacy Guide

Emergency Fund:
An emergency fund is money reserved for unexpected expenses or temporary income disruption.

Savings:
Customers should build savings according to their financial capacity and existing obligations.

Cash Flow:
Monthly cash flow can be estimated by subtracting monthly spending and required obligations from monthly income.

Positive Cash Flow:
Positive cash flow means income is greater than regular expenses and obligations.

Negative Cash Flow:
Negative cash flow means regular expenses and obligations exceed income. In this situation, reducing unnecessary spending and improving financial stability should generally receive priority over taking additional debt.

Debt Management:
Customers should understand their existing debt obligations before taking additional borrowing.

EMI:
An EMI is a regular payment made toward a loan. Customers should consider whether the EMI is affordable relative to their income and existing obligations.

Financial Stress:
High financial stress may indicate difficulty managing expenses, debt, savings, or income stability.

Financial Health:
Financial health reflects the customer's overall financial position, including income, spending, savings, debt burden, and stability.
