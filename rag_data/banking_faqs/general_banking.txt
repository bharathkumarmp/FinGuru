FinGuru Banking FAQ

Account Balance:
A customer can review their available account balance and recent transactions through the banking application.

Transaction Review:
Customers should review unfamiliar transactions promptly and report suspicious activity through the bank's official support or security channel.

Digital Banking Security:
Customers should never share passwords, PINs, OTPs, CVV numbers, or authentication codes with another person.

Beneficiary Changes:
Adding a new beneficiary can create additional transaction risk. Customers should verify beneficiary details before transferring funds.

Suspicious Transactions:
If a transaction appears suspicious, the customer should contact the bank and follow the bank's transaction-security process.

Financial Advice:
Financial recommendations should consider the customer's income, spending, savings, debt burden, financial health, financial stress, and affordability.

Responsible Recommendations:
A financial product should not be recommended merely because a customer may qualify for it. Suitability and affordability should be considered first.
