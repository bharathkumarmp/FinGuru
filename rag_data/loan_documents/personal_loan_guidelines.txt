FinGuru Personal Loan Guidelines

Loan Suitability:
A personal loan should be evaluated based on affordability, repayment capacity, existing obligations, income stability, and financial condition.

EMI Affordability:
Before taking a loan, a customer should assess the proposed EMI together with existing financial obligations.

Negative Cash Flow:
Customers with persistent negative monthly cash flow should not be encouraged to take additional borrowing without first addressing affordability and financial stability.

Financial Stress:
Elevated financial stress should be considered when evaluating additional debt.

Repayment:
Customers should understand the interest rate, tenure, EMI, total repayment amount, fees, and applicable terms before accepting a loan.

Responsible Lending:
Loan recommendations should prioritize customer suitability and affordability rather than maximizing product sales.

Simulation:
A loan simulation can estimate the proposed EMI and its impact on monthly cash flow and financial health. A simulation is not itself a loan approval.
