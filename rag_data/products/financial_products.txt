FinGuru Financial Products

Savings Plan:
A savings-oriented product can help customers systematically allocate part of their available surplus toward savings.

Emergency Fund:
An emergency-fund solution is intended to help customers build reserves for unexpected expenses or temporary income disruption.

Investment:
Investment products may be considered for customers with sufficient financial stability, positive cash flow, and capacity to tolerate investment risk.

Personal Loan:
A personal loan provides borrowing for eligible purposes. Suitability depends on affordability, repayment capacity, existing obligations, and financial condition.

Insurance:
Insurance products provide financial protection against specified risks subject to the product's terms and conditions.

Debt Support:
Debt-support guidance focuses on improving repayment capacity, managing obligations, reducing financial stress, and restoring sustainable cash flow.

Financial Education:
Financial education can help customers understand budgeting, savings, debt management, cash flow, and responsible financial decisions.
